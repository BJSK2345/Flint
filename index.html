What Flint actually does

Applies to your real PC

Wallpaper, accent colour & title-bar tint, light/dark mode, cursors, system sounds and desktop icons on Windows — and a full colour scheme on KDE.

Real interactive preview

Try a pack on a simulated desktop before you commit — drag windows, open the Start menu, hover the actual custom cursors, hear the sounds.

Undo with one click

Every install snapshots your current setup first, then applies changes step by step. Changed your mind? Restore Previous Setup puts it all back.

Safe by design

Packs are declarative data, never code — no scripts, no injection, no admin. Windows theming uses documented, reversible settings only.

Make your own

Build a pack in the creator, tune its generated artwork live, drop in your own cursors and assets, and export a shareable .pcpack.

Offline & free

Ships with a full catalogue of distinct packs. Works with no network and no account, and never takes a cut of a creator's price.
